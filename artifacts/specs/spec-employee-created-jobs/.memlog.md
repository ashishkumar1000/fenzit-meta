- (direction) User: 'task' means the existing job entity. Employee-created task = job with a creator and an approval state.
- (decision) Employee may create jobs for existing customers and may also create new customers (owner-created customer flow unchanged).
- (decision) Two owner-set switches per employee: may-create-own-jobs (on/off) and needs-owner-approval (on/off). No company-wide approval switch.
- (decision) Employee-created job is visible to its creator (assignee) and the owner; other employees do not see it.
- (decision) Approval off: job is assigned to the creator immediately and appears on the creator dashboard.
- (decision) Approval on: job is created in pending state. Pending is not counted as assigned and is not active work; creator dashboard shows it as Pending owner approval.
- (decision) Owner gets push and inbox entry when a job is submitted for approval.
- (decision) Owner may approve, or reject with or without a reason. Approve = assigned to creator; reject = not assigned, creator sees Rejected plus reason if given.
- (decision) Creator gets push and inbox entry on approve and on reject.
- (decision) Notifications: push plus an in-app inbox list with read state, for both owner and employee.
- (decision) Reports: an extensive dashboard for owner (company-wide) and employee (own numbers), on web. Must include employee-created, approved, auto-approved, owner-created counts; add pending, rejected, rates, timings and breakdowns (catalog in companion reports-dashboard.md).
- (decision) Platforms: web (fenzit-web) and mobile (fenzit-app) both, for owner and employee.
- (decision) Epic shape: one new epic. Deploy order: fenzit-be first (additive), then fenzit-app and fenzit-web independently.
- (assumption) Owner cannot edit a pending job's fields before deciding; owner only approves or rejects. (Not confirmed by user.)
- (assumption) Owner-created jobs are tagged creator=owner for reporting; no approval step applies to them.
- (assumption) Settings changes apply to new jobs only; jobs already pending keep their state.
- (constraint) Terminology: user-facing copy says Employee; DB role value technician and API fields stay unchanged.
- (constraint) Owner = company_admin membership role. Platform super admin is not an owner and has no company job actions.
- (constraint) Backend is the owner of approval rules and state transitions; clients never decide approval or visibility.
- (constraint) Approve and reject must be atomic and idempotent: a second decision on the same job returns a conflict, never a second notification.
- (constraint) Company isolation: all reads and writes are scoped to the job's company; cross-company access is denied.
- (question) Does an employee see their own rejected jobs on the dashboard, and for how long?
- (question) Can the owner see the approval queue filtered by employee and age?
