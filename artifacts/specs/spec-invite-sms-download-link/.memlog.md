- (decision) First name rule: take first word; transliterate non-English letters to English; cut to 12 characters; empty or unconvertible name uses 'there'. Logic lives in fenzit-be
- (decision) SMS failure does not roll back the invite; owner sees SMS not sent and can tap Resend
- (constraint) DLT template must be approved before SMS is switched on; max 3 variables; rendered message must fit one SMS (160 chars)
- (constraint) Cross-repo order: fenzit-be first (additive), fenzit-web download page deployed before the SMS feature is switched on

- (question) Rate limit reading: per employee phone number (assumed); confirm
- (question) Company name longer than 20 characters: restrict at company setup, or warn owner in invite form, or only truncate in SMS? Not decided

- (decision) Rate cap: 5 invite SMS per employee phone number within a rolling one-hour window (supersedes total-cap reading). Closes the rate-limit question
- (decision) Only company admin can press Resend. Viewers and employees cannot
- (decision) Re-invite of the same phone counts against the 5-per-hour cap
- (decision) Audit log of sent SMS (phone number, time, outcome) is acceptable and is used to enforce the cap
- (decision) Phone numbers are masked in logs (e.g. +91 ******3210). The audit log keeps a keyed hash of the number so the cap can count it; the masked form is for display and logs. Masking alone would merge different numbers, so the cap must not count on the masked form
- (direction) Owner will supply the approved DLT template ID later; SMS sending stays off until then
- (decision) Retention: audit records (masked number, time, outcome) kept 12 months then deleted; cap rows (keyed hash, time) kept 24 hours then deleted. Retention stated in privacy policy and Play Data safety form. Owner accepted the recommendation; legal check still advised
- (decision) Hash placement: the keyed hash lives only in the 24-hour cap rows and is deleted with them. The 12-month audit record keeps the masked number, time and outcome, never the hash. Supersedes CAP-6 wording that put the hash in the same record
- (decision) Both retention periods run from when the record is created (covers sent, failed and refused attempts)
- (decision) Privacy policy role example lists employee, admin or viewer
- (decision) Privacy policy ships with the SMS feature, not before
