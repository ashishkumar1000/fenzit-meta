## Leads

- Asymmetry is the core design fact for the RN decision: Android ships a native one-tap recovery dialog (startResolutionForResult); iOS ships none — the OS silently returns kCLErrorDenied and the app must build its own "open Settings" deep-link UX. Neither platform's services-off recovery can be satisfied by the permission button (CLLocationButton grants authorization but cannot flip the master toggle).
- accuracyAuthorization (iOS 14 reduced-accuracy / precise-location toggle): its doc page 404'd on the fetched endpoint this run — exact name/availability unverified here.
- iOS "provisional App While Using": no evidence retrieved this run; unverified.
- "Android 12 combined toggle" item: not evidenced this run; unverified.
- Exact FusedLocationProviderClient behavior with the master toggle off needs an authoritative source; Google Issue Tracker #256046114 notes RESOLUTION_REQUIRED behavior varies across play-services versions.
- The iOS no-system-alert claim rests on secondary sources; Apple's "Requesting authorization to use location services" page is the likely primary confirmation for round 2.
- AOSP LocationManager (non-Play) requestLocationUpdates behavior when the master toggle is off was glimpsed but not captured verbatim.
- developers.google.com reference pages do not support the .md endpoint that developer.android.com does; fetch them as full HTML next time.
