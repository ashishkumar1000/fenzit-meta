## E. CADENCE, DELIVERY, INTEGRATIONS

25. Standard cadence: attendance cut-off (~20th–25th) → payroll run 26th–30th (LOP/arrears/deductions) → pay date 1st–7th of next month. Zoho docs: default pay date 1st of following month, attendance lock ~5 days before pay date, then payroll lock freezing components. RazorpayX/Saral: cut-off 20th, process 21st–30th, credit 1st–7th. Some pay month-end same-month with tighter locks. [medium · convention-practice]
26. Legal pay-date constraint: Payment of Wages Act 1936 s.5 — before 7th of following month (≤1,000 employees), before 10th (larger). EPF/ESI/TDS are calendar-month based → early cut-offs need month-end adjustment logic. [medium · convention-practice]
27. Bulk bank transfer advice table-stakes at SMB (finding 13). TDS return filing (24Q e-TDS with FVU validation) at greytHR/factoHR; Keka only "quarterly reports". Accounting sync (Tally/Zoho Books): not named on the four pages read directly — unverified until product pages read. [high · product-feature]
28. Payslip immutability/versioning: no source states it. Keka "fix without rerun" implies correction-without-reissue semantics; no product advertises versioning. [absence]
