## Q1 — Governing wage law — VERDICT: CHANGES DESIGN
1. All four labour codes in force nationwide from 21 Nov 2025; CoW repeals PWA 1936, Minimum Wages Act, Bonus Act, Equal Remuneration Act. — DLA Piper (9 Feb 2026) + PIB/payroll.org/EY snippets. [high · regulatory]
2. CoW "employee" is universal (s.2): any person employed on wages by an establishment — no factory limitation. Field technicians are CoW employees. Factories Act never applied to a services business; state S&E Acts continue mainly for registration/working conditions. — PRS bare text. [high on definition; medium on S&E residual · regulatory]
3. Pre-CoW baseline: PWA applied only to factories/railways/industrial establishments (s.1(4)) and only to employees earning <₹24,000/month (s.1(6)). — whatishumanresource full act. [high · regulatory]

## Q2 — Wage slip fields — VERDICT: NEEDS LEGAL CAUTION
4. PWA contains NO wage-slip provision — the "PWA s.17(3)" premise is NOT supported by the bare act (s.13A registers; s.17 appeals). [high · regulatory]
5. CoW s.50(3): "Every employer shall issue wage slips to the employees in such form and manner as may be prescribed"; exemption only ≤5 employees for agriculture/domestic (s.50(4)). — PRS bare text. [high · regulatory]
6. Mandated field list comes from RULES, not the Act; operative slip form not retrieved. Search snippets reference a Form II under Code on Wages (Central) Rules 2020 (employer name/address, employee name/designation/code, days paid, wage detail incl. deductions, net payable, time-rate/piece-rate, nature of employment) — LEAD, not finding.
7. Central final rules under all four codes notified 8 May 2026 (KPMG Flash Alert 2026-127, snippet); state rules uneven (DLA 9 Feb 2026: Karnataka/Maharashtra/Kerala finalised; Delhi partial; some states still awaited). [medium · regulatory]
→ Build payslip as extensible per-state field sets; verify target states' Form text before freeze.

## Q3 — Pay date — VERDICT: CHANGES DESIGN
8. CoW s.17(1): monthly wages payable before expiry of the 7th day of the succeeding month — NO 1,000-employee split in the operative text. s.17(2): F&F within 2 working days of removal/resignation. — PRS bare text. [high on text; medium on numbering · regulatory]
9. Pre-CoW PWA s.5: <1,000 → 7th; ≥1,000 → 10th; terminated within 2 days. — whatishumanresource. [high · regulatory]

## Q4 — Deductions cap — VERDICT: CHANGES DESIGN
10. PWA s.7(3): ≤50% per wage period (75% only for co-operative-society payments); s.7(2)(b) absence permitted; s.9 caps absence deduction at proportionate wage. — Indian Kanoon + whatishumanresource. [high · regulatory]
11. Neither PWA s.7(3) nor CoW s.18(3) excludes absence-from-duty from the cap — the earlier "exclusion" assumption is NOT supported by the operative texts. [high on these two documents · regulatory]
12. CoW s.18: flat 50% cap — 75% co-op band GONE; permitted deductions include income-tax/statutory levies and PF/pension/health contributions; s.18(4) excess recovered as prescribed; s.18(5) employee not liable for employer's failure to deposit. — PRS bare text. [high · regulatory]
→ v1 should enforce a 50%-of-wage-period-gross total-deduction guard, LOP included, with an excess-recovery pathway rather than silent over-deduction.

## Q5 — LOP divisor — VERDICT: CONFIRMS DESIGN
13. Practitioner consensus (all 2026): divisor for ordinary LOP of salaried staff is a POLICY choice (calendar/30/26/working days) set in payroll policy; 26 mandated only where specific laws require a per-day rate (min wage/gratuity/encashment). — NeevHR (27 Oct 2026), groviaos (3 Nov 2026), attendmitra, jobmyindia (25 Sep 2026). [medium · regulatory-adjacent]
14. No retrieved statutory text imposes a divisor on ordinary LOP of CoW-governed salaried employees. [absence-of-evidence · regulatory]
