- (decision) Attendance is optional, owner-enabled per employee or for all; separate flow, no link to jobs
- (decision) Employee = existing technician role (incl. office staff); no new role in v1
- (decision) Owner can have multiple offices; each employee assigned to exactly one office; travel/site work out of scope
- (decision) Check-in and check-out both required; both must be inside office radius; blocked outside with distance shown
- (decision) GPS accuracy worse than ~100 m rejected; offline check-in/out blocked
- (decision) Office has start time, end time, late cut-off (default 15 min), hours rules; office-level only, no per-employee override
- (decision) Late = check-in after start + cut-off; flag visible to owner and employee; flag only, no penalty
- (decision) Hours rules owner-set: >= full-day hours = full day, >= half-day hours = half day, below = absent; days worked is decimal
- (decision) One check-in/out pair per day; night shifts crossing midnight out of scope
- (decision) No check-out by midnight tenant time = Checkout missing, counted as half day, flagged on owner dashboard until corrected
- (decision) Owner can correct attendance manually with audit trail
- (decision) Single leave type, no quota; full day and half day (first/second half)
- (decision) Owner approve / reject (reason optional) / revoke (reason required, future dates only)
- (decision) Employee can cancel pending or approved leave before it starts; in-progress multi-day leave: cancel/revoke only remaining future days (split)
- (decision) Leave counts working days only; past-date leave allowed up to 7 days back and needs owner approval
- (decision) Owner can apply leave on behalf of employee: auto-approved, employee notified, same 7-day back-date limit, revocable
- (decision) Check-in on approved leave day: allowed, day Present, leave for that day auto-cancelled, owner notified; past leave on day with check-in blocked
- (decision) Half-day leave + work: 0.5 leave + 0.5 worked, hours rule halved; no late flag/check-in reminder on first-half leave; no checkout reminder/early flag on second-half leave
- (decision) Weekly offs: tenant default Sunday, per-employee override wins; effective-dated, history unchanged
- (decision) Fixed holidays apply to all employees; can be added/removed for past dates with status recalculated; holiday over approved leave frees that day and notifies employee
- (decision) Working on weekly off/holiday allowed, shown as Worked on holiday
- (decision) Day statuses: Present, Half day, Absent, Leave, Half-day leave, Weekly off, Holiday, Worked on holiday, Checkout missing, Not tracked
- (decision) Enable mid-month: earlier days Not tracked; disable keeps history read-only; office assignment changes effective-dated
- (decision) Owner view: in-app monthly grid + per-employee summary; export later; no salary/month lock in v1
- (decision) Notifications in-app only (existing notifications table); design must allow push later via pushed_at outbox
- (decision) Reminders: employee not-checked-in at start+cutoff, not-checked-out at end time + actual minutes late; owner daily not-checked-in summary and pending leave count
- (decision) Timezone: tenant-level field, default Asia/Kolkata, all offices share tenant timezone
- (decision) Owner onboarding wizard: offices, timings/cut-off/hours rules, weekly offs, holidays (optional), select employees + assign office; employee onboarding: intro, location permission, office/timings/weekly offs screen
- (decision) Early checkout allowed and flagged; hours rules decide status
