## Full description (≤4000 chars)
Fenzit is the work app for field service teams. Assign jobs, track
attendance, and manage leave — so every job, technician and customer stays
on one simple record your whole team can trust.

BUILT FOR OWNERS AND SUPERVISORS
• Create jobs in seconds: pick the customer, the skill and the technician,
  and see every job from scheduled to done.
• Follow progress live: each job shows its workflow steps, notes and photos
  from the field.
• Know who is in: attendance check-ins with office geofence, late markers
  and worked hours — without chasing anyone on chat.
• Approve or reject leave requests with one tap, on behalf of anyone.
• Keep customers handy: every customer with their sites and job history.

BUILT FOR TECHNICIANS
• A clear today list: what to do, where to go, who the customer is.
• Move each job through its steps, add photos and notes from the site.
• Check in and out at the office or customer site — location is read only
  when you check in.
• Apply for leave and see the answer right in the app.

SIMPLE AND MADE FOR INDIAN FIELD TEAMS
• Sign in with your phone number and a one-time code. No passwords.
• Works on Android phones your team already has.
• Every company's data is kept separate and private.
• Photos and signatures are stored privately and open through links that
  expire.
